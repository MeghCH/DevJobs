# Wireframe Page Descriptions
![Acceuil first screen](/assets/accueil.png)
### 1. Home Screen
The **DevJobs** homepage is designed with a clean, minimalist aesthetic to ensure a seamless user experience. The interface features a harmonious color palette that is easy on the eyes. Key elements include a login button for profile access and a central search bar allowing users to filter opportunities by region and industry. A motivational slogan, **"Push your career,"** is prominently displayed in the center to inspire job seekers.

![Profil admin](/assets/profil-admin.png)

### 2. Admin/Company Profile
The Admin Profile is specifically tailored for companies. It provides recruiters with a clear overview of their active job postings and the applications they have received. While maintaining the visual consistency of the site, this page allows employers to manage their profile details (name, contact info, location) and view candidate summaries, including technical skills and background descriptions. A navigation bar provides quick access to the homepage, job listings, and the "Post a Job" feature.


![Publication des offres](/assets/publie-offres.png)
### 3. Job Posting Page
This page is dedicated to employers for creating job listings. The interface offers a structured form to ensure all essential information is captured: job title, company name, contract type, location, and remote work options. Additionally, recruiters can specify compensation, required experience, and key technical skills. A dedicated section allows for a detailed description of the role and missions, as well as an optional "Ideal Profile" description. A "Publish" button makes the offer live for all users.

![Description d'une offre d'emploi](/assets/descri-offre.png)

### 4. Job Description (User View)
Once a job is published, it appears to candidates with a clear layout showing the job title, company name, and location. The page provides a concise summary of the role, followed by the core missions and the desired candidate profile. A "Benefits" section is included to highlight the perks of the position, helping the offer stand out and encouraging candidates to apply.


![Profil user](/assets/profil-user.png)

### 5. User Profile
The Job Seeker’s profile is distinct from the recruiter’s view. Users can input their personal information and highlight their technical expertise to improve their visibility during the application process. The dashboard allows them to track their application history and view "saved" jobs for future consideration. A toggle feature also allows users to indicate they are "Open to Opportunities."


![Offres possibles](/assets/offres.png)


### 6. Job Listings & Search Results
After setting up their profile, users can browse available job offers. The platform includes filtering options by contract type and sorting features (from most recent to oldest). Each listing provides a brief summary, and a "Favorite" button allows users to save interesting roles to their profile to apply later or conduct further research.

![Description d'une candidature](/assets/candidature.png)
### 7. Application Process
When a user finds a matching role, they can apply directly through a dedicated section below the job description. The application form requires personal details and allows for the upload of a CV, cover letter, and any additional supporting documents. The process is finalized by clicking the "Apply" button.


![Motivation du user](/assets/motivation.png)


### 8. Candidate Review (Recruiter View)
This view represents the recruiter's perspective when reviewing an application. It consolidates all the candidate's information—including their cover letter, technical skills, and profile details—into a single professional overview. Recruiters can then use the "Accept" or "Reject" (Suitable/Not Suitable) buttons to manage their pipeline, decide on potential interviews, or move forward with the selection process.
