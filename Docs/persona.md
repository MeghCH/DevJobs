
# Persona: Lucas
- **Age**: 21 years old
- **Occupation**: Student at the end of his formation in devellopement
- **Tech proficiency**: High - social media native, prefers mobile-first interactions

## Background & Current Behavior
Lucas is completing his degree and is at a crossroads. While many of his peers are applying for Master’s programs, he has decided to enter the workforce immediately to gain financial independence. However, he feels completely lost in the traditional job market. He finds mainstream job boards (like LinkedIn or Indeed) intimidating, cold, and cluttered with senior-level roles.

He is part of a generation that consumes information in bite-sized, visual formats. The idea of writing long, formal cover letters for every application feels outdated and exhausting to him. He spends hours on his phone but struggles to find a professional tool that speaks his language.

## Goals
- **Secure an entry-level job** without needing two more years of study.
- **Find clear, honest job descriptions** that explain the daily reality of the role.
- **Simplify the application process** to avoid "form fatigue."
- **Get quick feedback** from employers to reduce job-search anxiety.

## Motivations
- Eager to start his professional life and earn a salary.
- Values efficiency and "no-nonsense" communication.
- Wants to work for a company that values potential and motivation over just a degree title.

## Pain Points
- **Experience Paradox**: Frustrated by "junior" ads that require 2+ years of experience.
- **The "Black Hole" Effect**: Sending out CVs and never hearing back, even a rejection.
- **Interface Friction**: Finds traditional corporate platforms boring and difficult to navigate on mobile.
- **Lack of Direction**: Doesn't know exactly which keywords to search for to find "starter" jobs.

## How Our Application Addresses Lucas's Needs
* **Discovery Feed**: Instead of complex search bars, he can browse opportunities via a familiar scrollable feed.
* **Profile-as-a-CV**: His digital profile acts as his resume, allowing for "one-click" applications.
* **Junior-Focused Filter**: The app prioritizes roles that don't require a Master's degree or extensive experience.
* **Direct Messaging**: Allows him to ask quick, informal questions to recruiters, lowering the barrier to entry.

# Persona: Sarah
- **Age**: 34 years old
- **Occupation**: Founder & CEO 
- **Tech proficiency**: Medium to High - uses digital tools for management and growth

## Background & Current Behavior
Sarah runs a fast-growing company and needs to hire regularly to keep up with demand. She doesn't have a dedicated HR department, so she handles recruiting herself. She is frustrated by traditional recruitment methods that feel like a "numbers game"—she receives hundreds of generic resumes that don't fit her company culture.

She is looking for a more proactive and modern way to find talent. She believes that the best candidates are often young, tech-savvy people who might not be looking on old-fashioned job boards. She wants a tool that allows her to "scout" talent rather than just waiting for applications to roll in.

## Goals
- **Find motivated talent** who fit the company culture.
- **Directly reach out** to interesting profiles without middle-men.
- **Save time** by quickly filtering through candidates based on skills and personality.
- **Build a modern employer brand** that appeals to Gen Z.

## Motivations
- Speed of hire to ensure business continuity.
- Finding a "human fit" rather than just a list of skills on paper.
- Reducing the administrative burden of traditional hiring.

## Pain Points
- **Time Sink**: Reading through hundreds of identical, AI-generated cover letters.
- **Ghosting**: Candidates who stop responding or don't show up for interviews.
- **High Costs**: Expensive job postings that often yield low-quality results.
- **Visibility**: Struggling to compete with big corporations for the attention of young graduates.

## How Our Application Addresses Sarah's Needs
* **Proactive Outreach**: Instead of waiting, she can browse a "talent pool" and send direct messages to users like Lucas.
* **Simplified Posting**: She can create a job "announcement" in minutes directly from her phone.
* **Verified Skills**: Clear, visual profiles help her see a candidate's strengths at a glance.
* **Direct Interaction**: A chat-based interface allows her to vet candidates' personality and responsiveness instantly.

---

## Two Ways of Using the App

To serve both Lucas and Sarah, the app provides two distinct experiences:

### 1. The "Explorer" Experience (For Lucas)
* **The Intentional Scroll**: A vertical or horizontal feed of job "cards" or short videos. 
* **Instant Notifications**: He gets a ping the moment a recruiter views his profile or sends him a message.
* **Career Guidance**: A simple dashboard that suggests roles based on his studies and interests.

### 2. The "Recruiter" Experience (For Sarah)
* **The Talent Scout Mode**: A dedicated dashboard where she can filter users by "available now" status and send direct "Work with us" invitations.
* **Brand Stories**: Sarah can post short updates or videos of her office/team to attract candidates organically.
* **Candidate Pipeline**: A simple "drag-and-drop" system to move candidates from "Interested" to "Interview" to "Hired."
